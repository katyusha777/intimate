## Why we built Intimate

The world of adult dating and escort services online is vast, but rarely beautiful, rarely safe, and almost never built with respect for the people who use it. Outdated ad sites, fake profiles, shady intermediaries: we felt it could be done differently. Intimate is our answer. A Dutch platform where advertisers and visitors find each other directly, without the noise, without middlemen, and without compromising on privacy.

## Verified, or it doesn't go live

Trust here isn't a slogan but a system. Every advertiser on Intimate goes through identity and age verification before an ad goes live. When you see a verification badge, you *know* there's a real person behind the profile: checked, of legal age, and in control of their own work. That's how we protect visitors from fake profiles and advertisers from identity abuse.

## Discreet by design

Privacy isn't a setting you switch on; it's in the foundation. We don't sell your data, we don't show tracking ads, and we collect only what's needed to make the platform work. Messages run through the platform, so you only share your private details when *you* choose to.

## Everything in one place

Intimate is more than an ad site. You'll find ads from verified escorts across the Netherlands, a marketplace for content and products, and soon Spaces: our own community where advertisers and visitors meet in a new way. All in one account, on desktop and mobile.

## Early access: build it with us

We're launching Intimate in early access, and that means something. Advertising is free right now, including daily boosts to put your ad at the top. In return we ask just one thing: your feedback. This platform is built *together* with the community that uses it, and the people who are here from the start will reap the rewards the longest.
