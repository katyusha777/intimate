Working as a sex worker can be a rewarding pursuit, both financially and socially. It offers opportunities to meet interesting people and to work in a variety of settings, but it also comes with unique risks. That's why it's crucial for a sex worker to work carefully and safely.

## Communication and setting boundaries

Make sure all services, rates and boundaries are clearly communicated and agreed upon before you confirm a booking. A 'safe word' can help signal boundaries during a meeting. Stand firm on your boundaries and make clear that you have non-negotiable conditions.

## Protecting your privacy

Use a work phone or work email to keep your private life separate from your work. Never share personal information such as your real name, home address or bank details.

## Safety at the meeting

Always arrange first meetings in public places where you feel safe and where other people are nearby. Arrange your own transport to and from the meeting location. Always let someone know where you'll be, with whom, and how long you expect to be away. Consider having a friend or a security service call or message you at set times to check that everything is okay.

## Health and hygiene

Have regular health check-ups and maintain good hygiene to protect both yourself and your clients. Insist on using protection to prevent transmissible conditions.

## Legal awareness and support

Be aware of the local laws around sex work and comply with the regulations. Make sure you have the contact details of legal support in case you need it.

## Financial security

Consider a system of upfront or partial upfront payment so that you're not entirely dependent on payment after the service is complete. Manage your finances wisely and make sure you don't end up in a vulnerable financial position.

The safety of a sex worker comes above all else. By following these guidelines, sex workers can take steps to minimize their risks and keep their work experience positive.
