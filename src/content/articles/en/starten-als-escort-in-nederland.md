Taking the step to work as an escort is an important decision that should be well considered. In the Netherlands, where the profession is legal, there are specific rules, regulations and resources that can support you as a new escort.

## A good start: research and preparation

Before you start out as an escort, it's important to research what the work involves. Get to grips with the legal aspects, the day-to-day reality, and the personal and emotional implications of the work.

## Understand the legislation

In the Netherlands you must work within the frameworks of the law as an escort. This means meeting certain conditions and possibly applying for a permit, depending on how you work. Check the legislation through official sources such as the Rijksoverheid (Dutch central government) website, or contact your local town hall for specific information.

## Safety comes first

Your physical and emotional safety are of the utmost importance. It's crucial to know how to stay safe, both in terms of health and personal security. Set boundaries and make sure you know how to leave a situation if you feel uncomfortable.

## Health

Regular medical check-ups are essential in the escort industry. Fortunately, the Netherlands has an excellent healthcare system, and organizations such as Soa Aids Nederland (the Dutch STI/AIDS foundation) offer plenty of information and help.

## Setting up your service

If you work independently, you'll have to find your own clients and do your own marketing. This can be through your own website, a network, or ads on specialized platforms.

**Marketing:** Develop a professional online presence. Privacy is key here; consider a pseudonym and be careful with personal information.

**Finances:** As a self-employed worker you're also an entrepreneur. That means filing tax returns and keeping your administration in order. Consider an accountant or use reliable bookkeeping software.

**Network and support:** Connect with other professionals in the field. Forums, communities and organizations for sex workers can offer support. P&G292, an Amsterdam-based organization, for example provides advice and support to sex workers.

## What to avoid

**Exploitation:** Stay alert to signs of exploitation and human trafficking. Your freedom to make your own choices is essential.

**Illegal services:** Steer clear of unregulated elements of the industry and report suspicious activity.

## Support organizations

- **Soa Aids Nederland** — for information and help around sexual health.
- **P&G292** — for support specifically for sex workers in Amsterdam.
- **PROUD** — the Dutch advocacy union for sex workers.
- **Shop Den Haag** — an organization offering support and advice to sex workers.

## In closing

A career as an escort can be rewarding, but it requires good preparation and knowledge of the field. Make sure you understand the legal frameworks, look after your health, protect your privacy and build a solid financial foundation. With the right preparation and support, you can build a safe and successful career in the escort industry.
